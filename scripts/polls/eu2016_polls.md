# 2016 EU referendum: vote by demographic group

Provenance: every number below was pulled from the cited source with an LLM-backed web-fetch tool that turns pages and PDFs into text. Rows marked [raw] were checked against the raw text of the PDF lines. Rows without [raw] came from the tool's table extraction and could hold transcription errors. No number here is invented or derived, except where a note says so.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
## 1. Lord Ashcroft Polls: "How the United Kingdom voted on Thursday... and why"

- Article: https://lordashcroftpolls.com/2016/06/how-the-united-kingdom-voted-and-why/
- Full tables (PDF): http://lordashcroftpolls.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/How-the-UK-voted-Full-tables-1.pdf
- n = 12,369 people who had voted (5,949 Remain / 6,420 Leave). The article says respondents were surveyed after they voted on referendum day. The PDF cover gives fieldwork as 21-23 June 2016, which includes postal voters. Online plus phone.
- Q.2 "Which way did you vote". Percentages are column % among voters. The weighted base / 12,369 is that group's share of voters in the weighted sample.
