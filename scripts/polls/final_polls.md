# Final polls before each vote

Each polling company's last published poll with fieldwork ending in the final 7 days before the
vote, transcribed from Wikipedia's "Opinion polling for the ..." pages on 9 November 2026 (URLs
below). Figures are as printed. Columns are party ids as used on the site. "?" means the page
printed a question mark; blank means no figure. Exit polls are left out. Where a page lists two
final polls from one company, both are kept as printed.

Headline figures as published: most pollsters exclude don't-knows, so shares sum to about 100.
For the referendums some pollsters print an "undecided" column; we keep it (col "und") and
compare on shares of those who picked a side.
