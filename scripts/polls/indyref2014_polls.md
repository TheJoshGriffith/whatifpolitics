## 2. YouGov referendum-day recontact poll, 18 Sept 2014 (secondary source only)

- YouGov article: https://yougov.co.uk/news/2014/09/18/yougov-referendum-prediction (https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/10489-yougov-referendum-prediction): 1,828 people interviewed on the day after voting, plus 800 who had already voted by post. Prediction: Yes 46, No 54.
- YouGov's own demographic tables were NOT found: the old archive link is dead. The figures below are % Yes as reported by John Curtice, What Scotland Thinks, "So Who Voted Yes and Who Voted No?": https://whatscotlandthinks.org/2014/09/voted-yes-voted/ . That post describes the sample as about 2,500: 1,756 on-the-day voters plus 783 earlier postal voters. No group bases are given.

| Group | YouGov % Yes | Ashcroft % Yes (as quoted by WST) |
|---|---|---|
| Men | 51 | 47 |
| Women | 42 | 44 |
| 16-24 | 49 | 52 (WST combines Ashcroft 16-17 and 18-24) |
| 25-39 | 55 | - |
| 40-59 | 47 | - |
| 60-64 | 45 | - |
| 65+ | 34 | 27 |
| ABC1 | 41 | 44 |
| C2DE | 50 | 47 |
| Born in Scotland | 49 | - |
| Born in England/Wales/NI | 26 | - |
| Born outside UK | 41 | - |
The WST post prints the age band as "60-64", which is odd. Check it against YouGov's original if you need it.

Same post, Ipsos MORI final polls (% Yes; averages across final polls, no n):
- ABC1 44, C2DE 50
- 20% most deprived neighbourhoods 65; most affluent fifth 36
- National identity: Scottish not British 88; More Scottish than British 65; Equally 26; More British than Scottish 9; British not Scottish 13

## 3. Turnout by age: ICM post-referendum survey for the Electoral Commission

- Source: What Scotland Thinks, "So How Many 16 and 17 Year Olds Voted?": https://www.whatscotlandthinks.org/2014/12/many-16-17-year-olds-voted/
- ICM telephone survey, n = 1,852, 19 Sept to 26 Oct 2014. Turnout is self-reported. The sample's non-voter share (16%) matched the official result.

| Age | Reported turnout % | n |
|---|---|---|
| 16-17 | 75 | 112 |
| 18-24 | 54 | not stated |
| 25-34 | 72 | not stated |
| 35-54 | 85 | not stated |
| 55+ | 92 | not stated |

No vote-by-group breakdown from this survey was found. No turnout figures by sex or class were found.
