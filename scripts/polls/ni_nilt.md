# Northern Ireland: party support and vote by group (Northern Ireland Life and Times Survey)

Provenance: every number below was pulled from the ARK "NILT" results pages with an LLM-backed web-fetch tool and spot-checked with a second fetch. Columns are % of respondents in the group. Nothing is invented. `*` = sample too small to report. Row labels are lightly normalised across years (e.g. "Sinn Féin" and "Sinn Fein", "Other party (specify)" and "Other Party", "(None of these)" and "None of these") so one parser reads them all.

- Publisher: ARK (Queen's University Belfast and Ulster University), Northern Ireland Life and Times Survey. About 1,200 adults a year.
- POLPART2 "Political party support" asks which party people support (all adults, including "none" and "don't know"). It is not a vote question, so we only use it to tell how groups differ from each other: the model fits every seat to its real result anyway.
- VOTEGE19 (NILT 2020) asks which party people voted for at the December 2019 general election (voters only).
- BREXVOTE (NILT 2016) asks how people voted in the 2016 EU referendum (voters only).
- The survey also reports a breakdown by religion. It is transcribed here for the record; the site doesn't use it.
