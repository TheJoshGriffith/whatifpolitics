# UK general elections 1992, 1997 and 2001: demographic breakdowns

Transcribed verbatim from Ipsos (then MORI) "How Britain Voted" pages, fetched 2026-11-09.
Region, telephone, trade union and newspaper rows are left out (we don't use them), as are the
change-since-last-election columns. Everything else is exactly as printed. "n/a" means the page
gives no figure. The pages give no share-of-voters column, so scripts/historic.py estimates each
group's size (see the note there).
