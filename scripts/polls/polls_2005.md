# UK General Election 2005: demographic breakdowns

## 1. Ipsos MORI: "How Britain Voted in 2005"

- Source: https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk/how-britain-voted-2005 (page also at https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk/node/171536), published 16.05.05.
- Base: 17,959 GB adults aged 18+; voters = "absolutely certain to vote" (10,986).
- Fieldwork: aggregate of MORI campaign surveys for the Observer, Financial Times, Sun, Evening Standard and Sunday Mirror.
- Only Con / Lab / LD / Other are given. No ethnic group section on the page.
- "Elect. %" = profile of the electorate; "Voters %" = profile of voters. Changes are since 2001.
- Check: every "Lab lead" equals Lab % minus Con % (verified by scripts/historic.py).
