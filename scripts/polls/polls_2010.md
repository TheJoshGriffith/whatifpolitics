# UK General Election 2010 — demographic breakdowns

## 1. Ipsos MORI — "How Britain Voted in 2010"

- Source: https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk/how-britain-voted-2010 (published 21.05.10)
- Base: 10,211 GB adults aged 18+, of whom 5,927 were "absolutely certain to vote" or said they had already voted.
- Fieldwork: 19 March – 5 May 2010; aggregate of all Ipsos MORI campaign voting-intention surveys.
- Weighting: at regional level to the final election result and turnout, and to the population profile.
- Note: Ipsos 2010 table has only Con / Lab / LD / Oth (no separate UKIP, Green, SNP/PC). Ethnic row label is "All non-white" (not "BME").
- Column super-headers: "Voting" (Con..Turn-out) and "Change since 2005" (last five columns).
