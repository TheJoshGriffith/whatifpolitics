# UK General Election 2015 — demographic breakdowns

## 1. Ipsos MORI — "How Britain voted in 2015"

- Source: https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk/how-britain-voted-2015 (dated 26.08.15); also https://www.ipsos.com/ipsos-mori/en-uk/how-britain-voted-2015?view=wide
- Base: 9,149 GB adults aged 18+, of whom 6,202 were "absolutely certain to vote" or said they had already voted.
- Fieldwork: 10 April – 6 May 2015; 3,196 telephone + 5,953 face-to-face interviews, aggregated across the campaign.
- Weighting: voting intentions weighted to the final actual results and turnout at regional level.
- Column super-headers: "Voting" (Con..Oth) and "Change since 2010" (last five columns). Header units as published: Con %, Lab %, LD %, UKIP %, Green (no unit), Oth (no unit), Con lead over Lab ± %, Turn- out, Con ± %, Lab ± %, LD ± %, Turn- out, Con- Lab swing %.
- No group-size / "% of all" / base column on the page; no separate SNP/PC column (included in Oth presumably — not stated). No computer-tabulation PDF linked from the page.
