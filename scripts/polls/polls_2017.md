## 1. Ipsos MORI — "How Britain voted in the 2017 election"

- Source: https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk/how-britain-voted-2017-election (charts PDF: https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/2017-06/how-britain-voted-in-the-2017-election_2.pdf)
- Base: 7,505 GB adults aged 18+, of whom 5,255 classified as likely voters (same definitions as previous elections).
- Fieldwork: telephone and online, 21 April – 7 June 2017.
- Weighting: party shares and non-voters weighted to actual results by region; data weighted to GB population profile. Turnout estimates to be treated with particular caution. EU referendum rows updated 4pm 20/06/2017 (per page).
- "*" = less than 0.5%. Blank = not published for that row (no change-since-2015 columns for 18-34s/35-54s/55+s by class, qualifications, 2015 vote, EU ref vote).
- Parties published: Con, Lab, LD, UKIP, Other only (no separate Green/SNP/PC).
- Turnout: two columns — "Turnout (all - recommended)" = per the PDF "% estimated turnout among all adults" (resident adults); "Turnout (all registered)" = among those registered.
- No group-size / "% of all" / base column on the page or PDF.
